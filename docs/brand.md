# CyberEinstein Brand System

CyberEinstein should feel like serious scientific infrastructure with a visible frontier—not a generic chatbot, a paper-summary product, or science-fiction decoration.

## Brand idea

**Core concept:** the evidence chain becomes a frontier.

The visual system connects two ideas:

1. **History is executable.** Claims, code, runs, evidence, failures, and reviews form an inspectable chain.
2. **Research moves forward.** That chain converges on a luminous frontier: the next justified scientific step.

The logo therefore combines an open **C-shaped evidence chain** with a **frontier star**. The chain is deliberately incomplete: CyberEinstein is not a closed body of knowledge. Every validated result extends it.

## Message hierarchy

| Level | Message |
| --- | --- |
| Category | Evidence-native AI scientist |
| Owned concept | Executable History of Science |
| Product promise | Inherit how science reached today’s frontier, then conduct the next verifiable study |
| Narrative enemy | Blank-prompt science: generating novelty without inheriting the field’s executable history |
| Trust promise | A result is not valid because an Agent says so; it must carry evidence, falsification conditions, and independent review |

## Taglines

Primary English:

> Inherit every step science has taken. Then take the next one.

Short descriptor:

Rules:

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the diameter of one evidence node.
- Do not rotate the chain, recolor individual nodes, or replace the frontier star with an atom icon.
- Do not put the transparent mark directly on a busy image.
- The hero is the preferred README entry point; the standalone wordmark is the preferred partner lockup.

## Typography

- Preferred UI and documentation family: `Inter`.
- System fallback: `ui-sans-serif`, `-apple-system`, `BlinkMacSystemFont`, `Segoe UI`, `sans-serif`.
- Chinese fallback: `PingFang SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`, `Noto Sans CJK SC`, `sans-serif`.
- Use compact uppercase labels only for system roles such as `RECONSTRUCT`, `VALIDATE`, and `DISCOVER`.
- Headlines may be ambitious; body text remains precise and calm.

## Diagram language

- Rounded scientific objects represent durable state, not temporary chat messages.
- Solid connections represent executed or evidenced relationships.
- Dashed rose connections represent failures, counterevidence, or paths returning to history.
- The gold frontier star appears only for a selected opportunity or justified next step.
- Avoid generic robot heads, glowing brains, Einstein portraits, DNA stock imagery, and decorative atom symbols.

## Voice

CyberEinstein is ambitious, evidence-first, historically grounded, skeptical, and open.

Use:

- “candidate Claim,” “historically grounded,” “executable evidence,” “failed attempt,” and “independent review”;
- clear boundaries between implemented capability and product direction;
- exact public metrics with their denominator and limitations.

Avoid:

- “fully autonomous scientist,” “one-click discovery,” “guaranteed correctness,” and “human-free science”;
- claiming that all 100 papers were fully reproduced;
- formal open-source claims until the repository has an explicit license.
